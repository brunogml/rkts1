How to search

Just write the consonants in the order they appear without "+", vowels or irregular ༹. (i.e. ཙ, ཚ, ཛ are ts, tsh and dz and for example ཀ༹ is k only)

For instance:

ཀུནས།→kns
སྒྲུབྱིས།→sgrbys
ཀླེུནད།→klnd
དཀྱིུང།→dkyng
བཀ༹ལ།→bkl
རྣཾསེ།→rnMs or rnms
