How notes work
Reading notes

Notes left by researchers are visible to everyone. Open the Notes panel with the blue tab on the right edge of the screen. Passages that have been annotated are highlighted in yellow in the text — click any highlight to jump to the corresponding note card.

Adding a note

Adding notes requires a registered account. To annotate a passage:

  1. Select the text you want to annotate by clicking and dragging.
  2. Click the + Add note button that appears in the toolbar.
  3. The Notes panel opens with an editor pre-filled with your selection and its folio position.
  4. Write your note — use the formatting toolbar for bold, italic, colours, highlights, and links.
  5. Click Save.
Editing & deleting

You can edit or delete your own notes at any time using the and buttons on each note card. Administrators can delete any note.
