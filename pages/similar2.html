How Similar Passages works
1 · Select a passage

In this view, the query was passed directly from the previous page. Use the Reload button to re-run, or adjust the mode toggle.

2 · Keywords are extracted

Grammatical particles are filtered out; the remaining syllables are your keywords, shown as yellow tags. Bigrams, trigrams and quadgrams are used silently in scoring.

3 · Search mode

Bag of words — keywords may appear anywhere in the 4-line window (default). Phrase order — keywords must appear left-to-right.

4 · Score

The score 6/8 means 6 of your 8 keywords were found in that passage.
